# 6. Exporting & Distributing Your Game

### Portable Build
Outputs `YourGame.exe` alongside an encrypted `game.bpack` archive (or loose `GameData/` folder).

### Windows Installer (Setup.exe)
Configure Version, Publisher, Welcome Message, per-language Terms & Privacy Policy, Sidebar Photo, and Desktop/Start Menu shortcuts. Includes a clean `Uninstall <Game>.exe`.
